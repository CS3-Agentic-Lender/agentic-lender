# Design: AL-333

## Design decision

Use a council on a dark stage for the committee and a 3D chain in the Loan note. Voting pillars, a Macro observer, centre recommendation and policy-gate ring sit beside text tabs. The chain highlights only this loan's two records.

## Alternatives considered

The facts record several previews, including orb and table options. Nikoloz chose "Council on the dark stage" and a chain rather than a brass coin he disliked.

## Why this was chosen

The selected layout distinguishes agent roles and represents stored loan records. The facts record the choice but no further personal preference rationale. Nikoloz needs to supply that if required for his interview.

## Implementation limits

three.js 0.170.0 loads lazily. Scenes pause off screen, release WebGL and respect reduced motion. Text retains the facts without WebGL. Phones can scroll over the stage.
